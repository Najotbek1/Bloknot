## English (en-us)

**Short description:**
```
Daily, weekly and monthly plans, reminders, notebooks, a focus timer and statistics — fully offline.
```

**Description:**
```
Maqsad helps you organise your plans and actually get them done.

• Daily, weekly, monthly, dated and open-ended plans; repeating tasks
• Reminders even with the window closed — the app keeps running in the tray
• Calendar: the month and the selected day side by side
• Notebooks: note list beside the editor, autosave, search
• Focus timer (Pomodoro): 15–60 minutes, pause, breaks
• Drag plans into your own order
• Statistics and a responsibility score; a coach that speaks to your discipline
• Sync with your phone over Wi-Fi with a QR code — no internet, no server, encrypted
• 6 themes, 8 languages, keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+N, Ctrl+1…5)

All data stays on your computer. No account, no ads.
```

**Search terms (≤ 7):** `planner`, `to-do`, `reminders`, `pomodoro`, `notes`, `calendar`, `habit`
